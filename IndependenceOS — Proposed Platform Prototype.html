Build a Business That Performs Without You.

IndependenceOS turns the Exit Ready Strategy journey into a working digital environment: measure owner dependency, build independent management, and prove it before a buyer does.

One journey, four stages

1
Assess

Owner dependency, structure and financial readiness.

2
Build

Roles, delegation, decision authority and SOPs.

3
Verify

Owner-absence testing and financial evidence.

4
Prepare

Due diligence, buyer readiness and exit options.

Four ways a business depends on its owner

Decision

Approvals, pricing and hiring that wait for one person.

Knowledge

Processes and terms that live in the owner’s head.

Relationship

Customers and partners owned personally, not by the company.

Behavioral

Teams that pause until the owner weighs in.
